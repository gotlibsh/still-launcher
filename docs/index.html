Still

A quiet, text-only home screen for Android.

What it is

No icons, no badges, no feed. Just the time, the weather, a note to yourself, and a handful of apps as plain words. Everything else is one swipe away.

On the home screen

  • Weather for the cities you choose, with a full forecast a tap away. No location access.
  • Clock and date. Tap for alarms or your calendar.
  • A short note, and a link to your real notes.
  • Optional Google Home switches for a few devices, like an AC or a boiler.
  • Up to eight favorite apps.

Gestures

  • Swipe up for all apps, with search ready.
  • Swipe down for notifications.
  • Swipe left or right to open apps you choose.
  • Long-press for settings.

Privacy

No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
